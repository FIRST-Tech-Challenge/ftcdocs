Volunteer Resources
===================

Welcome, :term:`volunteers <Volunteer>`! *FIRST* Tech Challenge events cannot
happen without you. Most of FTC Docs is written for teams, but the resources
on this page are written for the volunteers who plan and run events.
Resources are organized by volunteer role.

Most *FIRST*-created volunteer documents can be found on the
`ftc-resources <https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org>`__
website, but there are some documents that are community-maintained and those
documents live here. Just click on the button for the resource you want to
explore!
