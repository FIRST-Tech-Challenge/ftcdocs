Budget and Shopping List
========================

The prices on this page are what volunteers have paid in recent seasons. They
change often, so treat them as a guide for budgeting rather than a quote.
Several regions buy whenever prices dip, rather than all at once.

All of the tables assume the event already has a computer, plus the
microphones, speakers, and cables it uses for the in-person event.

Equipment You'll Reuse
----------------------

Some purchases pay for themselves over many events, and others don't:

- **Good to buy:** IP cameras, PoE switches, stands, network cable, and
  microphones. They're used every season and rarely break.
- **Good to borrow first:** mixers, speakers, projectors, and laptops. Schools
  and venues often have them, and you may find you don't need your own.
- **Store it together:** keep each field's equipment in its own rolling tote
  (about $100) with a printed packing list. See
  :doc:`Day-of Checklist and Troubleshooting </event_streaming/day_of_checklist/day-of-checklist>`.
