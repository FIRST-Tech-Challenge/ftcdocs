Robot Controller Mounting Best Practices
------------------------------------------

Controlling your ROBOT requires that your ROBOT CONTROLLER and DRIVER STATION
are able to talk to each other. Teams are strongly encouraged to mount their
ROBOT CONTROLLER away from noise generating devices such as motors. Teams
also avoid putting EMF shielding materials like sheets anywhere between the
ROBOT CONTROLLER and DRIVER STATIOM. Refer to the specific documentation for
your devices to determine where the WiFi radio is.
